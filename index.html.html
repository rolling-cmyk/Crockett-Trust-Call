Trust & transparency

Policies & safety.

Draft product policies for review—not legal advice and not a substitute for a California privacy/counsel review before launch.

18+ eligibility

Crockett Trust Call is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old. You must enter your accurate date of birth and confirm you are an adult to create an account. We may suspend or remove accounts where age eligibility is in doubt. Self-reported age is not conclusive age verification; launch requires an appropriate age-assurance process that minimizes the data collected.

Community standards & reporting

Be respectful and honest. Harassment, threats, scams, impersonation, non-consensual intimate content, and attempts to evade a block are prohibited. Members can report or block another account. Reports should be reviewed by a trained safety team; urgent threats should be directed to local emergency services. A report form alone is not active moderation.

Privacy: photos, messages, and AI coaching

In a production service, collect only data needed to provide and protect the service. Clearly disclose how profile details, approximate location, photos, messages, and coach conversations are stored, processed, shared with service providers, retained, and deleted. Do not use private conversations or uploaded photos to train models without separate, informed permission. Provide account access, correction, export, deletion, and consent controls as applicable under California law. Define retention periods and secure deletion, including backups, before launch.

This demo stores profile and coaching preferences locally in your browser and does not send them to a server. Uploaded picture previews remain in browser memory. Do not enter sensitive personal information in a demo. Real account deletion, data export, and server-side retention controls are not connected.

Subscriptions & billing

Crockett Plus is planned at $13.99 USD per month plus applicable taxes, with no free trial. Before purchase, production checkout must disclose renewal frequency, total due, tax, cancellation terms, and how to cancel. Members should be able to change payment methods, see invoices, and cancel through self-service billing. No payment processor is connected here.

California privacy notice

Before production, publish a complete California-compliant privacy notice describing categories and purposes of personal information, retention, disclosures/sale or sharing practices, sensitive information, consumer rights and request methods, verification procedure, appeals where required, and an accessible contact channel. Assess California age-appropriate design, online dating, auto-renewal, consumer privacy, and other applicable requirements with qualified counsel. This concise draft is not a compliance determination.

Security

Production requires secure authentication, optional two-factor authentication, access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, secure image handling, abuse monitoring, breach response, and vendor due diligence. Never store raw card data in the dating app; use a PCI-compliant payment provider. Set a clear process for account recovery and suspected compromise.
